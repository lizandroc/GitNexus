## 1. What the page actually sells

Two distinct motions. **Every prospect gets routed to one of them — the sequence, the
persona and the offer all differ.**

### Motion A — Provider Partnership Program
- **Who:** medical weight loss clinics · telehealth prescribers · independent practices
- **Mechanic:** the provider *recommends* the test; the patient orders through the myLAB Box
  provider portal; the provider **earns on every kit and every reorder**
- **Why it compounds:** monitoring cadence is every 3–4 months → **3–4 orders per year, per patient**
- **What they get:** revenue with no inventory, adds clinical credibility, keeps patients
  engaged between visits, orders and revenue tracked in the portal

### Motion B — Enterprise & Wholesale (Volume Buyers)
- **Who:** telehealth platforms · employers & benefits programs · pharmacies · health systems
- **Mechanic:** volume pricing on bulk kit orders, **direct-to-patient fulfillment** to their population
- **What they get:** digital physician-reviewed results, dedicated account support for rollout

**Shared to both:** zero inventory, zero fulfillment, **no minimums or commitments**,
pricing tailored to expected volume, response within one business day.

## 2. The timing hook (the page's own, and it's the strongest one)

**Medicare's GLP-1 Bridge launched July 1, 2026.** Eligible Part D members get Foundayo,
Wegovy (injection or tablet) or Zepbound (KwikPen) for a **$50 copay per 30-day supply**,
administered centrally by CMS outside the normal Part D payment flow, running through
**December 31, 2027**.

**Why this is the wedge:** it puts a large, previously priced-out population onto GLP-1s.
Every one of those patients needs baseline and periodic monitoring, and the organizations
serving them are about to feel the volume. Partners who add a structured monitoring
protocol *now* are ready for it; partners who wait are retrofitting under load.

**Secondary hook (use for pharmacies and clinics):** the compounding era is closing. FDA
resolved the semaglutide and tirzepatide shortages (503A/503B grace periods ended
Feb–May 2025), and on **April 30, 2026** proposed removing semaglutide, tirzepatide and
liraglutide from the 503B Bulks List entirely. Programs that competed on cheap compounded
supply need a new margin line and a new differentiator. A monitoring panel is both.

> Use one hook per message, not both. Bridge for volume/enterprise; compounding for
> pharmacies and clinics whose economics just changed.

## 5. Segments

Mapped to the landing page's own organization-type picker, so inbound and outbound use
one taxonomy.

| ID | Segment | Page org type | Motion | Tier | Sequence |
|----|---------|---------------|--------|------|----------|
| S1 | Medical weight loss clinics & med spas | Medical weight loss clinic | A | 3 | SEQ-A |
| S2 | Telehealth platforms | Telehealth platform | A or B | 2 | SEQ-B |
| S3 | Pharmacies (retail, independent, compounding) | Pharmacy | B | 2 | SEQ-B |
| S4 | Employers & benefits programs | Employer / benefits program | B | 1 | SEQ-C |
| S5 | Health systems | Health system | B | 1 | SEQ-C |
| S6 | Gyms, clubs & wellness operators | Other | B | 2 | SEQ-B |

### S1 · Medical weight loss clinics & med spas — *Motion A*
- 1–10 locations, owner-operated, cash-pay; the volume segment
- **Personas:** Owner / Medical Director · Practice Manager
- **Triggers:** advertising a GLP-1 or weight program, new location, hiring an NP
- **Pain:** GLP-1 is their fastest-growing line, but they have no lab workflow; sending patients to a draw site loses the sale
- **Lead:** V1 → V4 · **Cycle:** 2–6 weeks

### S2 · Telehealth platforms — *Motion A or B*
- DTC/telehealth, 1k–100k active members. Route to B once volume clears 500 kits/mo
- **Personas:** Head of Product · VP Clinical · Head of Growth · Founder
- **Triggers:** launching/expanding a weight vertical, funding round, state-licensure expansion
- **Pain:** activation drop-off between consult and first dose; needs clinical credibility to differentiate
- **Lead:** V4 → V6 → V2 · **Cycle:** 2–4 months
- Most product-literate segment — lead with the integration and a real number

### S3 · Pharmacies — *Motion B*
- Independents, regional chains (3–50 stores), 503A/503B facilities repositioning
- **Personas:** Owner / Pharmacist-in-Charge · Director of Clinical Services
- **Triggers:** adding clinical services, the 503B bulks proposal, point-of-care testing announcements
- **Pain:** the compounding line has a visible end date and needs replacing with something durable
- **Lead:** V5 → V2 · **Cycle:** 2–4 months

### S4 · Employers & benefits programs — *Motion B, Tier 1*
- 5,000+ covered lives; employers, TPAs, benefits platforms, ICHRA administrators
- **Personas:** VP Total Rewards / Benefits Director · CMO · Head of Clinical Partnerships
- **Triggers:** publishing a GLP-1 coverage policy, adding a weight-management vendor, RFP cycle
- **Pain:** GLP-1 is the fastest-growing line in plan spend; pharmacy claims show fills, not appropriateness or safety
- **Lead:** V2 → V6 · **Cycle:** 4–9 months

### S5 · Health systems — *Motion B, Tier 1*
- IDNs, regional systems, employed-physician groups with weight-management service lines
- **Personas:** Service Line Director, Weight Management · VP Ambulatory Ops · CMIO
- **Triggers:** launching a bariatric/weight service line, a population-health initiative, the Bridge
- **Pain:** lab capacity and patient no-show rates on outpatient draws; Bridge volume landing on existing clinics
- **Lead:** V6 → V2 → V4 · **Cycle:** 6–12 months
- Longest cycle, highest ceiling. Expect procurement, IT security review and a BAA.

### S6 · Gyms, clubs & wellness operators — *Motion B*
- Multi-site clubs, HVLP chains, medical-wellness hybrids
- **Personas:** VP Wellness/Health Services · Chief Product Officer · Head of Member Experience
- **Triggers:** announcing a clinical or GLP-1 partnership, launching a wellness vertical
- **Pain:** wants clinical credibility without becoming a clinic; retention is the business model
- **Lead:** V6 → V7 · **Cycle:** 3–6 months
- Routes to "Other" on the form — tag manually so inbound matches outbound

## 6. Qualification

Score 0–2 per line. **7+ = SQL** · 4–6 = nurture · ≤3 = disqualify.

| # | Qualifier | 2 points | 0 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Live GLP-1 or medical weight program | Running and advertised | "Exploring it" |
| Q2 | **Monthly kit volume** (the page's own bands) | 100–500 or 500+ | Under 25 |
| Q3 | Current lab workflow | Draw site, or no labs at all | Already has an integrated at-home vendor |
| Q4 | Named clinical owner | Medical director / PIC identified | No clinical decision-maker |
| Q5 | Motion fit | Clean fit to A or B | Wants something we don't do |
| Q6 | Budget authority reachable | Owner or VP+ engaged | Coordinator only |

**Volume bands** map directly to the landing-page form, so an inbound submission scores
itself: `Under 25 kits` · `25–100` · `100–500` · `500+`.

**Hard disqualifiers:**
- No licensed prescriber attached to the program
- Asks us to source, supply or broker medication
- **New York State only** — the GLP-1 test is temporarily unavailable in NY. Suppress
  NY-only organizations; for multi-state prospects, flag the NY limitation in the first call.
- Any request to bypass a prescriber relationship
